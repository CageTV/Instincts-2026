0.2.1
  - Taming prompt: the "Attempt to Tame" box is now recognised by its button label (not by the engine's internal callback type, which another mod that wraps message
    boxes can change). The second button, which used to repeat "Attempt to Tame", is always renamed "Cancel", also when the box skip could not take the box.

  - BeastHarmony.log lists every queued message box (buttons and callback type) for the first 30 boxes, to find out why a box was not taken.
