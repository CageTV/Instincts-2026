# Design note: racial taming and the Bosmer Green Pact (not built yet)

Idea from the author, 2026-11-15. For later, once the story line of the Milandriel project and the Instincts base are settled.

- **Races change taming.** Each playable race gets a small taming/animal-handling trait. (Fury - Animal Taming of Skyrim has per-race passives, for example the Bosmer's feeding is 15 % more effective; here only as a reference for the shape of the idea, the content is ours.)
- **Bosmer: the Green Pact.** Bosmer are bound to the beasts of the wild by the Green Pact:
  - taming and talking to animals is easier for a Bosmer (higher tame chance, fewer failures);
  - animals that are normally aggressive towards other races are not aggressive towards a Bosmer (the animal's faction/aggression check treats a Bosmer player as friendly);
  - the pact does not cover everything: no effect on undead, daedra, dragons or other non-beasts, and no free tamed pets.
- **Other races:** a smaller or different trait each (Khajiit with cats, Nords with bears and wolves, and so on), kept lore-friendly and mild.
- **Open questions:** how the "not aggressive" part is done without breaking Skyrim's AI (faction relations on the player vs. an ability that calms nearby animals), whether it ships in Beast Harmony (DLL) or the plugin, and how it interacts with the knock-out odds and the level gates.
